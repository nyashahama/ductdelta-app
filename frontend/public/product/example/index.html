One duct reroute

Fictional change CC-014 · Level 2 plant room

A beam blocks the planned supply duct route. Follow the five records from the site observation to the receipt and the amount still due.

Five linked records

The site team, the project-appointed issuer and finance each leave a record that the next person can use.

Explore the workflow

Field observation

The site lead opens OBS-014 with the Level 2 plant room location, the beam conflict and the available photo. The original drawing and field photo stay with the record while written direction is pending.

Instruction and notice

The project-appointed issuer issues SI-018 for the offset. The team links it to the duct affected by the instruction and records when notice NT-014 was sent and received.

Scope and quotation

The commercial team links the accepted R82,000.00 quotation Q-014 revision 1. It covers the changed route and its supports. The signed instruction and the signed price remain separate records.

Certification

After the team records the completed reroute, the signed certificate CERT-006 values the work at R82,000.00. The R4,100.00 retention leaves R77,900.00 payable.

Receipt allocation

Finance records receipt RCPT-021 and allocates R60,000.00 to CERT-006. R17,900.00 remains due and the change stays open.

R82,000.00 certified

The signed certificate values the completed reroute.

R4,100.00 retention

Retention is held separately. R77,900.00 is payable after retention.

R60,000.00 allocated

The receipt reduces the payable balance.

R17,900.00 still due

The change remains open. All amounts are in ZAR excluding VAT.

Why this change stays open

The reroute has been installed and certified. The commercial team still needs to resolve what is due and track the amount held as retention.

  • The receipt was allocated in full. The resulting R17,900.00 is still due.
  • The R4,100.00 retention stays separate from the amount currently payable.
  • If an adjustment or a dispute changes the balance, keep the decision and its evidence with the case.
