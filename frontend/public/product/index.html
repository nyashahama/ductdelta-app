Keep mechanical changes on record

DuctDelta helps mechanical contractors keep the site evidence, written instruction, agreed scope and payment on one change record.

See a worked exampleExplore the workflow

From a site finding to the outstanding balance

One record, from site to finance

Give each team the context it needs at the next handoff.

Site supervisors

Log what the crew found and where. Attach a photo or field note the office can use to review the change.

Project managers

Keep the issuer, the written direction and when notices were sent and received with the affected scope.

Quantity surveyors

Reconcile the agreed quantity, additions and credits with the work measured on site. Keep unresolved lines visible.

Finance teams

See what was certified, what was retained and where each receipt was applied. Keep the remaining balance in view.
