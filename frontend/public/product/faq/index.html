Direction and price

How the project team keeps authority, notices, and commercial agreement together.

The project agreement determines who can direct work. Permission to proceed, scope, price and time each have their own record.

Record when the notice was sent and received with the external references. Use the project agreement to determine authority and timing.

Keep the agreed scope and price with the signed quotation. Keep the remaining lines open or disputed so they stay visible during valuation and closeout.

Tie the addition or credit to the affected scope and its supporting records. Track the agreed change and measured progress separately so the commercial team can follow both.
